Let's Encrypt is a free, automated, and open Certificate Authority run by the Internet Security Research Group (ISRG). It was created with a single goal: to make encrypted HTTPS connections the default across the entire web. Netstreet automatically provisions free SSL certificates for all new hosting accounts — in most cases, you do not need to do anything at all. This guide explains how Let's Encrypt works on your Netstreet account, how to verify your certificate is active, and how to install or renew a certificate manually when needed.

<Info>
  Netstreet is transitioning new accounts from Let's Encrypt to **cPanel
  SSL** certificates. The two are equivalent in terms of trust level, validity,
  and encryption strength. The only visible difference is that your browser's
  certificate details will say "cPanel Inc" instead of "Let's Encrypt." Your
  site's security is unaffected.
</Info>

## How automatic SSL works

When you add a domain to your Netstreet hosting account, the system automatically:

1. Generates an SSL certificate for your domain (and the `www.` subdomain)
2. Installs the certificate on the web server
3. Renews the certificate automatically every 90 days

This process is completely transparent — you do not need to do anything. Certificates can take up to **four hours** to generate for a newly added domain. If your site urgently needs a certificate sooner, please contact Netstreet support via your [client area](https://my.netstreet.co.za).

<Note>
  Automatic SSL **does not** overwrite any existing paid SSL certificate already
  installed on your account. If you have purchased a certificate from a third
  party, it takes precedence and will be used in preference to the free
  certificate.
</Note>

## Installing Let's Encrypt via cPanel

If you need to verify your SSL certificate status or manually trigger installation on a cPanel account:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to cPanel">
    Access cPanel from your Netstreet Hosting Panel: go to **Products & Services → Hosting & Servers → Manage → cPanel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the SSL/TLS section">
    In the **Security** section of the cPanel home screen, click **SSL/TLS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View certificates on the server">
    Under **Certificates (CRT)**, click **Generate, view, upload, or delete SSL certificates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Issuer column">
    In the **Certificates on Server** list, look in the **Issuer** column. If Let's Encrypt (or cPanel Inc) is listed, your certificate is active and automatically renewing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Troubleshooting automatic SSL in cPanel

If a certificate has not been generated for your domain, the most common causes are:

<Accordion title="Another SSL certificate is already installed">
  If a valid, expired, or self-signed certificate is already installed on the
  domain, the automatic installer will skip it. Remove the existing certificate
  first, then allow up to four hours for the free certificate to be issued.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="URL rewrite rules are blocking certificate validation">
  Let's Encrypt validates domain ownership by placing a file in the
  `public_html/.well-known/acme-challenge/` directory. If your `.htaccess` file
  has URL rewrite rules that block access to this path, validation will fail.

  Add the following line to your `.htaccess` file to exclude the `.well-known`
  directory from rewrites:

  ```apache
  RewriteRule ^.well-known - [L]
  ```
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The domain does not resolve to the server">
  Let's Encrypt verifies that the domain resolves to your server's IP address
  before issuing a certificate. If your domain's DNS is not yet pointing to
  Netstreet's servers, wait for DNS propagation to complete (up to 48
  hours) before the certificate can be issued.
</Accordion>

## Installing Let's Encrypt via Plesk

If your VPS runs Plesk (available on legacy Managed WordPress accounts and self-installed on Unmanaged VPS):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Plesk" />
  <Step title="Navigate to Websites & Domains">
    In the left sidebar, click **Websites & Domains**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Let's Encrypt icon">
    Locate the Let's Encrypt plugin icon for your domain and click it. The **Let's Encrypt SSL Certificate** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type a valid email address. Let's Encrypt sends renewal reminders and important notices to this address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include the www subdomain (optional)">
    Select **Include www.yourdomain.co.za as an alternative domain name** if you want the certificate to cover both `yourdomain.co.za` and `www.yourdomain.co.za`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Install">
    Plesk will contact Let's Encrypt, complete domain validation, and install the certificate. When complete, you will see a success message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify SSL is enabled">
    Go to **Websites & Domains → Hosting Settings**. Under **Security**, confirm the **SSL support** check box is selected and the Let's Encrypt certificate is chosen in the **Certificate** list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Renewing Let's Encrypt in Plesk

Plesk renews Let's Encrypt certificates automatically once a month (certificates expire every 90 days, so this provides a comfortable buffer). To manually trigger a renewal:

1. Go to **Websites & Domains** → click the **Let's Encrypt** icon
2. Click **Renew**

## Installing SSL via Enhance Control Panel

If your Netstreet account uses the Enhance control panel:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the Hosting Panel">
    Go to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Websites">
    In the left sidebar, click **Websites**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your website">
    On the **Manage Websites** page, click the website you want to manage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Security tab">
    Click the **Security** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install a custom SSL certificate">
    In the **SSL Certificates** section, click **Install Custom SSL**. Then:

    - Select the **Domain** from the drop-down list
    - Paste your **Certificate (CRT)** text, or click **Upload file** to upload it
    - Paste your **Private Key** text, or upload the file
    - Click **Upload**

    The Hosting Panel installs the certificate for the selected domain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  In most cases with Enhance, you do not need to install an SSL certificate
  manually — the free SSL certificate is installed and renewed automatically.
  Use the manual procedure above only if you have purchased a paid certificate
  from Netstreet or a third-party provider.
</Note>

## Installing Let's Encrypt on an Unmanaged VPS (command line)

If you run an Unmanaged VPS at Netstreet, you manage SSL certificates yourself via the command line using **Certbot**.

<Warning>
  The Let's Encrypt documentation recommends using `snapd` for Certbot
  installation. However, `snapd` is incompatible with the Netstreet VPS
  infrastructure. Use your Linux distribution's package manager to install
  Certbot directly instead.
</Warning>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Ubuntu / Debian + Nginx">
    ```bash
    # Install Nginx and Certbot
    sudo apt install -y nginx certbot python3-certbot-nginx

    # Obtain and install the certificate
    sudo certbot --nginx -d yourdomain.co.za -d www.yourdomain.co.za
    ```

    Certbot will automatically update your Nginx configuration and set up a cron job for automatic renewal.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Ubuntu / Debian + Apache">
    ```bash
    # Install Apache and Certbot
    sudo apt install -y apache2 certbot python3-certbot-apache

    # Obtain and install the certificate
    sudo certbot --apache -d yourdomain.co.za -d www.yourdomain.co.za
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="AlmaLinux + Apache">
    ```bash
    # Install Certbot via EPEL
    sudo yum install epel-release
    sudo yum install certbot python3-certbot-apache

    # Obtain and install the certificate
    sudo certbot --apache -d yourdomain.co.za -d www.yourdomain.co.za
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Certbot sets up automatic renewal. Test the renewal process with:

```bash
sudo certbot renew --dry-run
```

For a full list of Certbot-compatible ACME clients, visit [letsencrypt.org/docs/client-options](https://letsencrypt.org/docs/client-options).

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="How long does it take for a new certificate to be issued?">
  On shared hosting accounts, certificates are usually issued within minutes.
  In some cases it can take up to four hours. If your certificate has not
  appeared after four hours, please contact Netstreet support.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Let's Encrypt work with all domains?">
  Let's Encrypt requires the domain to resolve to your server's IP address
  before it can issue a certificate. Domains that have not yet had their DNS
  updated, or that use a third-party proxy service that masks the origin IP,
  may not pass validation immediately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will my certificate renew automatically?">
  Yes. On cPanel and Enhance accounts, certificates renew automatically. On
  Plesk, Plesk handles renewal monthly. On an Unmanaged VPS with Certbot,
  Certbot installs a cron job that handles renewal automatically.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use Let's Encrypt for a wildcard certificate?">
  Yes. Let's Encrypt supports wildcard certificates (e.g. `*.yourdomain.co.za`),
  but they require DNS-based domain validation rather than the HTTP-based method
  used for standard certificates. This is typically handled via the Certbot
  `--manual` flag with DNS plugins on a VPS. Contact Netstreet support
  if you need assistance.
</Accordion>
