Your cPanel password is the key to your entire hosting account on Netstreet. It protects not only your cPanel login, but also your primary FTP account, MySQL database access, and SSH access. Changing it regularly — and choosing a strong password — is one of the simplest and most effective ways to keep your website and data secure.

<Warning>
  Your cPanel password controls access to your files, databases, and email. Never share it with anyone. If you need to give a developer or collaborator access to specific parts of your account, use the [Manage Team](/control-panels/cpanel/manage-team) feature to create a sub-account with limited permissions instead.
</Warning>

## Changing your cPanel password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to cPanel">
    Open cPanel through the Netstreet client area or directly at `https://yourdomain.com:2083`. If you need help logging in, see [Getting Started with cPanel](/control-panels/cpanel/getting-started).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Password & Security">
    On the cPanel home screen, scroll to the **Preferences** section and click **Password & Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    In the **Old Password** text box, type your existing cPanel password. This step verifies that you are the authorised account holder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter and confirm your new password">
    In the **New Password** text box, type the password you want to use. Then re-enter it in the **New Password (Again)** text box to confirm there are no typos.

    <Tip>
      Click the **Password Generator** button and cPanel will create a strong, random password for you. Copy it to a secure password manager (such as Bitwarden or 1Password) before you save. A generated password is far harder to guess than most manually chosen ones.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the new password">
    Click **Change your password now!** The new password is applied immediately — your active session remains open, but any other open sessions are ended.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  After saving a new password, make sure to update it in any FTP clients, email clients, or deployment tools that use your cPanel credentials, because they will fail to authenticate with the old password.
</Note>

## What makes a strong password?

cPanel includes a built-in strength meter that scores your password as you type. For a Netstreet account, aim for a password that:

- Is at least **12 characters** long (longer is better)
- Mixes **uppercase and lowercase letters**, **numbers**, and **special characters** (such as `!`, `@`, `#`, `$`)
- Does **not** contain your username, domain name, or easily guessable words
- Is **unique** — not reused from any other account

<Info>
  The **Password Generator** in cPanel always produces a password that meets these requirements. Using it takes the guesswork out of creating a secure password.
</Info>

## Resetting a forgotten cPanel password

If you cannot log in to cPanel because you have forgotten your password, you have two options:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Reset via Netstreet client area">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Log in to the client area">
        Go to [https://my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and sign in with your Netstreet account credentials (these are different from your cPanel credentials).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Go to your hosting account">
        In the left sidebar, click **Hosting & Servers**, then locate your account and click **Manage**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Change the password">
        Look for the **Change Password** or **Reset cPanel Password** option and follow the on-screen prompts to set a new password.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Reset via the cPanel login page">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Visit the cPanel login page">
        Go to `https://yourdomain.com:2083` in your browser.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click Reset Password">
        On the login page, click the **Reset Password** link below the login form.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Provide your details">
        Enter your cPanel username and the email address on file for your account. cPanel will send a password reset link to that address.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Follow the email link">
        Open the email and click the reset link. You will be prompted to choose a new password.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    <Note>
      If the **Reset Password** link does not appear on your cPanel login page, please open a support ticket through the Netstreet client area and our team will enable this option for you.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
