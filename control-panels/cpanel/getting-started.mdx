Welcome to cPanel, the control panel that powers your Netstreet Africa hosting account. Whether you are setting up your first website or managing an established online presence, cPanel gives you a single, browser-based interface to handle files, databases, email, domains, security, and much more — all without needing to touch a command line.

## Logging in to cPanel

There are two convenient ways to access your cPanel account through Netstreet Africa.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Via the Netstreet Africa Client Area">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in to your client area">
        Open your browser and go to [https://my.netstreetafrica.com](https://my.netstreetafrica.com). Enter your email address and password to log in.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Navigate to your hosting account">
        In the left sidebar, under **Products & Services**, click **Hosting & Servers**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open cPanel">
        Locate the hosting account you want to manage, click **Manage**, and then click **Login to Control Panel**. cPanel opens automatically in a new tab — no separate password needed.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Direct URL">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Construct your cPanel URL">
        In your browser's address bar, type `https://yourdomain.com:2083`, replacing `yourdomain.com` with your actual domain name. You can also use `https://cpanel.yourdomain.com` if that URL resolves for your account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your credentials">
        On the cPanel login page, enter your cPanel username and password, then click **Log in**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    <Tip>
      If you have forgotten your cPanel password, you can reset it from within the Netstreet Africa client area under **Hosting & Servers → Manage → Change Password**. Alternatively, on the cPanel login page, click **Reset Password** and provide your username and recovery email address.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Your cPanel username and password are separate from your Netstreet Africa client area credentials. Keep both sets of login details in a secure location.
</Note>

## What you can do in cPanel

Once logged in, the cPanel home screen greets you with a searchable grid of tools organised into sections. Here is a quick overview of the main capability areas:

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Files** | Upload, edit, and organise website files; manage FTP accounts; use disk space quotas |
| **Databases** | Create and manage MySQL databases and users; run queries via phpMyAdmin |
| **Domains** | Add addon domains, subdomains, and redirects; manage DNS zones |
| **Email** | Create email accounts, forwarders, autoresponders, and spam filters |
| **Security** | Install SSL certificates, set up two-factor authentication, manage SSH access |
| **Software** | Install applications (including WordPress via Softaculous), manage PHP versions |
| **Preferences** | Change your password, update contact information, add team users |

## Logging out of cPanel

When you have finished working in cPanel, always log out to protect your account — especially on shared computers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the user menu">
    In the top-right corner of the cPanel screen, click your username or the account icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Log out">
    Select **Log out** from the dropdown menu. You are returned to the cPanel login page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you close the browser tab without logging out, your session remains active until it times out. Always use the **Log out** option when you are done, particularly on public or shared devices.
</Warning>
