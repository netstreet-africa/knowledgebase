Plesk is a powerful web hosting control panel that lets you manage your websites, domains, email accounts, databases, and files all from a single browser-based interface. On Netstreet Africa, Plesk is available on certain legacy Managed WordPress accounts as well as unmanaged VPS and Dedicated Server plans. If you are unsure whether your hosting plan uses Plesk or cPanel, check the **Products & Services** section of your Netstreet Africa client area.

## What you can do with Plesk

Plesk gives you the tools to manage every aspect of your hosting from one place. Key capabilities include:

- **Website management** — add domains and subdomains, configure DNS zones, and manage website settings
- **File management** — browse, upload, edit, and organise files using the built-in File Manager
- **Database administration** — create and manage MySQL databases and users; run queries with phpMyAdmin
- **Email** — set up email accounts, forwarders, and spam protection for your domains
- **Security** — install SSL/TLS certificates (including free Let's Encrypt), manage SSH keys, and configure IP restrictions
- **Backups** — create manual or scheduled backups of your sites and restore them when needed
- **Users and access** — create additional user accounts with custom roles and restricted permissions

## The Plesk dashboard

After you log in, the Plesk dashboard is your starting point. It is divided into several key areas:

| Area | Description |
|---|---|
| **Left sidebar** | Navigation links to Websites & Domains, Email, Databases, Users, and more |
| **Central panel** | Overview cards for your hosted domains and quick-access links |
| **Right sidebar** | Resource usage summary, storage, and bandwidth at a glance |
| **Top navigation bar** | User account menu, notifications, and a log-out option |

## Logging in to Plesk

You can access your Plesk account in two ways:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Via the Netstreet Africa client area">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Log in to the client area">
        Go to [https://my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and sign in with your Netstreet Africa credentials.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Locate your hosting account">
        In the left sidebar, click **Hosting & Servers** under **Products & Services**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open Plesk">
        Find your hosting account in the list and click **Manage**, then click **Login to Control Panel**. Plesk opens in a new browser tab and you are logged in automatically.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Direct browser access">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Enter the Plesk URL">
        In your browser's address bar, type `https://yourdomain.com:8443`, replacing `yourdomain.com` with your actual domain name. You can also use your server's hostname: `https://serverhostname:8443`.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your credentials">
        On the Plesk login page, type your Plesk username and password, then click **Log in**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    <Tip>
      If you have forgotten your Plesk password, you can reset it from within the Netstreet Africa client area. Alternatively, see [Changing Your Plesk Password](/control-panels/plesk/logging-in) for instructions on resetting it from within Plesk once you are logged in.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Logging out of Plesk

Always log out when you are done managing your account, especially on shared or public computers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click your username">
    In the top navigation bar, click your username or account name in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Log out">
    Click **Log out** from the dropdown menu. You are returned to the Plesk login screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you simply close your browser tab without logging out, your Plesk session stays active until it times out. This creates a small but real security risk. Use the **Log out** option to end your session properly.
</Warning>
