The AI Website Builder for WordPress, powered by Extendify, takes you from a blank WordPress installation to a fully designed, content-filled website in just a few minutes. Instead of choosing a theme, configuring page builders, and writing placeholder content yourself, you answer a few questions about your business, select a design, and the AI generates a complete site for you. You can then refine the layout, edit the text, generate custom images, and publish — all from within the WordPress admin.

## How the AI Website Builder works

When you log in to a freshly created WordPress site on your Netstreet Africa hosting account for the first time, the AI Website Builder onboarding process starts automatically. You tell the builder what your site is about, choose a design, and it creates a full multi-page (or single-page) website with relevant content and imagery. After the initial build, you have access to a suite of AI-powered content tools to keep refining and expanding your site.

<Note>
  The AI Website Builder is available on Netstreet Africa WordPress hosting accounts. If the onboarding wizard does not start automatically, you can access it from the WordPress admin under the **Site Assistant** panel.
</Note>

## Initial site onboarding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your Netstreet Africa account">
    Go to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and log in. Under **Products & Services**, locate your WordPress hosting account and click **Login to Control Panel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your website">
    In the control panel, click **Websites** in the left sidebar, then click the site you want to set up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to WordPress">
    In the **Applications installed** section, locate your WordPress installation and click **Manage**, then click **Login to admin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the onboarding wizard">
    If this is the first time logging in, the AI Website Builder wizard starts automatically. On the **What type of website are you creating?** page, select the option that best describes your site (for example: Business, Portfolio, Blog, Online Store, or Non-profit) and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us about your website">
    On the **Tell Us About Your Website** page, enter your business or organisation name, a brief description, and any other relevant details. The more context you provide, the more relevant the AI-generated content will be. Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your site structure">
    Select whether you want a **Multi-Page Website** (multiple separate pages for Home, About, Services, Contact, etc.) or a **Single-Page Website** (all content on one scrollable page). Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a design">
    Browse the available design options and select one that matches the look and feel you want for your site. Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your pages">
    Choose which pages you want included in your site (for example: Home, About, Services, Contact, Blog). Tick the checkboxes next to the pages you want and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your new site">
    The AI Website Builder generates your complete site. When the build is complete, click **View site** to preview the result. You are now ready to customise the content.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Creating and editing content with AI tools

After the initial site is generated, the AI Website Builder provides a full set of AI-powered editing tools to help you create, refine, and manage content.

### Accessing the content editor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Site Assistant">
    In the WordPress admin, look for the **Site Assistant** panel. Click **Start Writing with AI** to open the page editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### AI content tools

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Design Library" icon="palette">
    Browse hundreds of pre-built page layouts organised by design type (About page, Blog post, Pricing page, Contact page, and more). Click any layout to apply it to your current page instantly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Page Generator" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Type a description of the page you want to create and let the AI generate a complete, ready-to-use page. Great for quickly adding new service pages or landing pages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Writing Tools" icon="pen-nib">
    Select any text on the page and use the AI Tools sidebar to fix spelling and grammar, simplify language, make text shorter or longer, change the tone, or generate entirely new text.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Image Generator" icon="image">
    Generate custom images based on a text description. In the content editor, type `/` and click **Image**, then describe the image you want and click **Generate image**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Using the Design Library

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Design Library">
    In the page editor, click the **Design Library** button in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by design type">
    Use the **Design Type** dropdown to filter layouts (for example, show only layouts for an "About" page or a "Pricing" page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a layout">
    Click any layout in the library to insert it into your current page. The layout is pre-filled with placeholder text you can then customise.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Using the AI Page Generator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the AI Page Generator">
    In the page editor, click the **AI Page Generator** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your page">
    Type a description of the page you want to create — for example: "A services page for a plumbing business in Johannesburg offering emergency repairs, pipe installations, and geyser servicing."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the page">
    Click **Generate Page**. The AI creates a full page with headings, body text, and a suitable layout based on your description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    Review the generated content, make any edits you want, and then click **Publish** in the top-right corner to make the page live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Using the AI writing tools

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select text">
    In the page editor, select the text you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an AI action">
    In the **AI Tools** sidebar that appears, select an action:
    - **Fix grammar** — corrects spelling and grammatical errors.
    - **Simplify language** — makes the text easier to read.
    - **Make shorter** — condenses the selected text.
    - **Make longer** — expands the selected text with additional detail.
    - **Change tone** — rewrites the text in a different tone (professional, friendly, formal, etc.).
    - **Generate new text** — replaces the selection with AI-written content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the change">
    Review the AI's suggestion and click **Accept** to apply it, or **Reject** to keep the original text.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Generating images

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the image generator">
    In the content editor, click the location where you want to insert an image. Type `/` to open the block inserter, then click **Image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate an image">
    In the right sidebar, click **Generate an image**. In the **IMAGE DESCRIPTION** field, type a detailed description of the image you want (for example: "A professional South African plumber working on pipes in a modern kitchen").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert the image">
    Click **Generate image**. The AI creates a custom image based on your description. Click **Insert** to add it to your page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Publishing your page or site

When you are satisfied with your content, publish it to make it live on your site.

- To publish a single page: click **Publish** in the top-right corner of the page editor.
- To update a page you have already published: click **Update** in the top-right corner after making your changes.

<Tip>
  After publishing significant changes, clear your site's cache (via your Netstreet Africa control panel or any caching plugin you use) so that visitors immediately see the updated content rather than a cached version.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Will the AI Website Builder overwrite my existing content?">
  The full site onboarding wizard only runs on a fresh WordPress installation. If you run it on a site with existing content, it may overwrite your current theme and pages. Use it on new installations only.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use the AI tools to create content in languages other than English?">
  Yes. Describe your site and provide prompts in your preferred language, and the AI will generate content in that language. Results may vary by language — English typically produces the most accurate and natural-sounding output.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I switch to a different design after the initial onboarding?">
  Yes. You can change your site's design at any time by installing a different WordPress theme under **Appearance → Themes**, or by using the Design Library to apply new layouts to individual pages.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a limit to how many AI-generated images I can create?">
  The number of AI image generations available depends on your hosting plan. Check the details of your specific Netstreet Africa plan in the client area for current limits.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I am not happy with the generated site?">
  You can regenerate individual pages or sections using the AI Page Generator, or manually edit any content using the standard WordPress block editor. You are not locked into the AI-generated output — it is a starting point you can freely customise.
</Accordion>
