Registering a domain name through Netstreet is straightforward and can be completed entirely through your client area. Whether you are launching a brand-new website or securing a domain for future use, this guide walks you through every step — from searching for availability to completing your registration.

## Before you begin

Before starting your registration, it helps to have a few things ready:

- A shortlist of domain names you want (availability is not guaranteed)
- Your registrant contact details (name, address, email, and phone number)
- A valid payment method on file in your client area

<Note>
  ICANN requires that all domain registrants provide accurate and up-to-date contact information. Providing false details can result in domain suspension.
</Note>

## Choosing the right TLD

A top-level domain (TLD) is the suffix at the end of a domain name — for example, `.com`, `.co.za`, `.net`, or `.africa`. The TLD you choose can affect how your domain is perceived by visitors and search engines. Netstreet offers a wide range of TLDs to suit different needs.

<Tip>
  If you plan to target a South African audience, registering a `.co.za` domain alongside your `.com` is a good way to protect your brand and improve local search visibility.
</Tip>

## Registering your domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your client area">
    Go to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and sign in with your credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to domain registration">
    In the top navigation or the left sidebar, click **Domains**, then select **Register a New Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for your domain">
    Type your desired domain name in the search box and click **Search**. The system will check availability across all supported TLDs and display the results.

    <Note>
      If your preferred `.com` name is taken, the search results will suggest available alternatives including different TLDs and slight name variations.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the domain to your cart">
    Click **Add to Cart** next to the domain (or domains) you wish to register. You can register multiple domains in a single order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your registration period">
    Choose how many years you want to register the domain for. Most TLDs can be registered for 1–10 years. Registering for multiple years means you will not need to renew as frequently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure domain privacy (optional)">
    Enable **Domain Privacy** (WHOIS Privacy) to replace your personal contact details in the public WHOIS database with generic registrar information. This reduces spam and protects your personal data.

    <Tip>
      Domain Privacy is strongly recommended, especially for personal projects or small businesses that do not want their home address publicly listed.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and complete your order">
    Review your order summary, confirm pricing, and click **Checkout**. Complete payment using your preferred method. You will receive a confirmation email once the domain is successfully registered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After registration

Once your domain is registered, it typically becomes active within a few minutes. You can then:

- **Point it to hosting** — Update the nameservers in your client area to direct the domain to your Netstreet hosting package.
- **Set up DNS records** — Use the DNS editor in your client area to add A, CNAME, MX, and other records.
- **Configure email** — Add MX and SPF records to route email to your chosen mail provider.
- **Enable auto-renewal** — Turn on automatic renewal so your domain does not accidentally expire.

<Warning>
  ICANN requires you to verify your registrant email address within 15 days of registration. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link. Failure to verify may result in your domain being suspended.
</Warning>

## WHOIS and registrant information

Your registrant contact details are stored in the WHOIS database and are tied to the legal ownership of the domain. You can update this information at any time from your client area.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your domain settings">
    Log in to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za), go to **Domains**, and click the domain you want to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update Registrant">
    On the domain details page, click **Update Registrant**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and save">
    Make your changes in the **Update Registrant** dialog box and click **Submit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Changes to registrant contact information may trigger a verification email to both the old and new email addresses. This is an ICANN-mandated security measure.
</Info>

## Internationalized domain names (IDNs)

Netstreet supports Internationalized Domain Names (IDNs), which allow you to register domains using non-ASCII characters — for example, domain names in Arabic, Chinese, Cyrillic, or other scripts. IDNs are converted to an ASCII-compatible encoding (ACE) format known as Punycode for use in the DNS system. When searching for an IDN, simply type the domain using the characters of your chosen language.
