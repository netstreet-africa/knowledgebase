Keeping your domain renewed is one of the most important things you can do to protect your online presence. If a domain expires, your website and email will stop working, and you risk losing the domain entirely if someone else registers it before you can renew. Netstreet makes it easy to renew your domains manually or to set them up for automatic renewal so you never have to worry about a lapse.

## Understanding domain expiry

When a domain registration period ends, the domain does not immediately become available to the public. Most domains go through a **grace period** of 30–45 days after expiry during which you can still renew at the standard price. After that, a **redemption period** may apply where recovery is possible but incurs additional fees. Eventually, the domain is **released** back to the open market.

<Warning>
  Do not let your domain enter the redemption period. Recovery fees can be significantly higher than a standard renewal, and not all domains can be recovered once released.
</Warning>

## Enabling automatic renewal

The easiest way to ensure your domain never expires is to enable automatic renewal. When this setting is active, Netstreet automatically generates a renewal invoice **14 days before the expiry date**, giving you enough time to ensure payment is processed before the domain lapses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your client area">
    Go to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your domains list">
    In the left sidebar, under **Products & Services**, click **Domains**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the domain">
    Click on the domain you want to configure for automatic renewal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Billing settings">
    In the left sidebar of the domain detail page, click **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle auto-renewal">
    Scroll down to the **Renewal & Subscription Settings** section. Click the **Auto-renew** slider to enable or disable automatic renewal. When enabled, the slider will appear active/highlighted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Enable automatic renewal for every domain you rely on — especially your primary business domain. It only takes a moment and can save you the headache of a website outage or expensive domain recovery.
</Tip>

## Renewing a domain manually

If you prefer to manage renewals yourself, or if you need to renew a domain immediately, you can do so at any time from your client area.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your client area">
    Go to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your domains list">
    In the left sidebar, under **Products & Services**, click **Domains**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the domain">
    Find the domain you want to renew. You can use the filter tabs — **Active**, **Cancelled**, **Pending Cancellation**, or **All** — to narrow down the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click on the domain">
    Click the domain name to open its detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Billing and pay the invoice">
    In the left sidebar, click **Billing**. Review the renewal invoice and complete payment using your preferred payment method.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can renew a domain before its expiry date. If you renew early, the additional time is added on top of the remaining registration period — you do not lose any time you have already paid for.
</Note>

## Renewing multiple domains at once

If you manage several domains, you can renew them all in a single transaction rather than renewing each one individually.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your domains list">
    Log in to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and navigate to **Domains** under **Products & Services**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select multiple domains">
    Use the checkboxes next to each domain you want to renew, then look for a bulk action or renewal option in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    Review the combined renewal invoice and complete payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens if a domain expires?

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Grace Period" icon="clock">
    **0–45 days after expiry.** Your domain stops resolving, but you can still renew at the standard price with no penalty.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Redemption Period" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    **45–75 days after expiry.** Recovery is possible but incurs a significant redemption fee on top of the renewal cost.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pending Delete" icon="trash">
    **75–80 days after expiry.** The domain is queued for deletion and cannot be recovered. After deletion it re-enters the public market.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  Exact grace and redemption period lengths vary depending on the TLD (top-level domain). Some country-code TLDs have shorter or no grace periods. Check with Netstreet support if you are unsure about a specific TLD.
</Info>

## Keeping payment details up to date

Automatic renewal only works if your payment method is valid and has sufficient funds. Make sure your billing details in the client area are always current.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit your account settings">
    Log in to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and go to your account or billing settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update payment method">
    Add or update your credit card, debit card, or other payment details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm billing details">
    Ensure your billing address matches the details on file with your bank to avoid payment failures.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If an automatic renewal invoice is generated but payment fails, your domain will not be renewed. Watch out for payment failure notifications from Netstreet and resolve them promptly.
</Warning>
