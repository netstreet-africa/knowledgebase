Managing your WordPress site in Enhance

## 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/managing-your-wordpress-site-in-the-enhance-control-panel#open-the-wordpress-management-page)

Open the WordPress management page

1. Log in to your Enhance control panel.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Websites**. ![](https://files.readme.io/228bef28a564fa447f4a92d5c208a9c69d68e7e5322737068090c99d2d6b34a5-image.png)
3. On the **Manage websites** page, click the website you want to manage.
4. In the **Applications installed** section, locate your WordPress site, and then click **Manage**. ![](https://files.readme.io/7f44bdd5a1e23d7b867af7b155bca07559aa9aee9e4c1a35a0d9156f8aab1110-image.png)

## 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/managing-your-wordpress-site-in-the-enhance-control-panel#manage-wordpress-settings)

Manage WordPress settings

### 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/managing-your-wordpress-site-in-the-enhance-control-panel#general)

General

The **General** section opens first.

- To log in to your WordPress dashboard, click **Login to admin**. ![](https://files.readme.io/5691943c7fe05e0d5448a1b7547618dc3a80744842101fabbd0355107c4ddb04-image.png) The dashboard opens in a new tab.
- In the **General** section, you can also:
 - Change the WordPress site URL.
 - Change the location of your WordPress site files.
 - Manage versions for your WordPress site.
 - Enable or disable maintenance mode.

### 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/managing-your-wordpress-site-in-the-enhance-control-panel#plugins)

Plugins

To manage your WordPress plugins, click **Plugins** in the left sidebar. ![](https://files.readme.io/e9f52fc291ae3156e3a7caae27f1d662dd3184bc93f10a64b1e6acd1ec57b168-image.png) In the **Plugins** section, you can:

- View installed plugins, including version and status information.
- Install, activate, deactivate, update, and delete plugins.

### 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/managing-your-wordpress-site-in-the-enhance-control-panel#themes)

Themes

To manage your WordPress themes, click **Themes** in the left sidebar. ![](https://files.readme.io/30cc950ce1993e3d2979f6c634ae15e102e085862fb0da725d23f099f77ff506-image.png) In the **Themes** section, you can:

- View installed themes, including version and status information.
- Install, activate, deactivate, update, and delete themes.

### 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/managing-your-wordpress-site-in-the-enhance-control-panel#security)

Security

To manage WordPress security settings, click **Security** in the left sidebar. ![](https://files.readme.io/6053309056f6d5465b8c3746b04069a148b76bd514d215e79113bc578215af68-image.png) In the **Security** section, you can restrict administrative access to your WordPress site by IP address.

### 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/managing-your-wordpress-site-in-the-enhance-control-panel#users)

Users

To manage your WordPress user accounts, click **Users** in the left sidebar. ![](https://files.readme.io/86ef56320d0f2e65bc73d1a56906632478fcd41754bca4b0e5077e339cc4d830-image.png) In the **Users** section, you can:

- Add a user account by clicking **Add user**.
- Modify an existing user account. You can change the username, email address, and password for an account.
- Delete a user account.

### 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/managing-your-wordpress-site-in-the-enhance-control-panel#single-sign-on)

Single sign on

To manage single sign on settings for WordPress, click **Single sign on** in the left sidebar. ![](https://files.readme.io/600851d469276268ac5f296ae1d4257be2436df8739e6c8fd6e6cf7b5a617404-image.png) With single sign on, you can log in to your WordPress site without typing a separate username and password.

### 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/managing-your-wordpress-site-in-the-enhance-control-panel#debug)

Debug

To configure WordPress debugging settings, click **Debug** in the left sidebar. ![](https://files.readme.io/b72a66826e96819e98871fee6808dbf68c0c00950a2dd1fccce302f104b0a151-image.png) In the **Debug** section, you can enable these WordPress debugging settings:

- **WP\_DEBUG**
- **WP\_DEBUG\_LOG**
- **WP\_DEBUG\_DISPLAY**

For more information about these debugging options, see [Debugging WordPress](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/debugging-wordpress).
