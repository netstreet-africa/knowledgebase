# Source: https://kb.hosting.com/docs/sending-limits-for-professional-and-business-email

To help maintain server performance and stability, Professional and Business email hosting accounts enforce a sending limit of **one thousand (1000) messages per hour**. This limit:

- Helps prevent abuse.
- Reduces the risk of IP address blacklisting.
- Ensures fair use for all of our customers.

If you have a large number of messages you want to send, try the following techniques to stay within the sending limit:

- Spread out sending your email messages over a longer period of time.
- Break up large recipient lists into smaller batches.
