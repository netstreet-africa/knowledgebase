# Source: https://kb.hosting.com/docs/dns-settings-for-professional-and-business-email

You can view the DNS settings for your Professional or Business Email account in the Hosting Panel. To do this, follow these steps:

1. Log in to the Hosting Panel at [https://my.hosting.com](https://my.hosting.com).
2. In the left sidebar, under **Products & Services**, click **Email & Office Tools**: ![](https://files.readme.io/0002e34cf844f26b9e1de4f78e2745aa9712bf0e7577a9b3009dd09e9ac4567b-image.png)
3. On the **Email & Office Tools** page, locate your email account, and then click **Manage**: ![](https://files.readme.io/c44e6ae3f43f8f4ae8a7062e3e714766319e84b79b89c6f199159270af2a2845-image.png)
4. On the **Overview** tab, information appears for the following DNS records:

 - MX
 - SPF
 - DMARC
 - DKIM
