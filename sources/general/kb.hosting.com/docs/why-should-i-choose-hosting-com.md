Reliability

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/why-should-i-choose-hosting-com#network-experience)

Network experience

We don’t wait for a network alarm (or worse, a customer) to tell us something is wrong before we fix something. Proactivity is one of the best traits to have in this field. We keep a close eye on bandwidth, computer processor load, memory load, and network traffic, and take steps to correct issues before they become a problem.

## 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/why-should-i-choose-hosting-com#competitive-pricing)

Competitive pricing

We admit we’re not the cheapest company out there, but “you get what you pay for” is more than just a cliche. We offer the lowest prices we can, while figuring in costs such as equipment, bandwidth, and highly-trained staff. We don’t offer many “specials,” offering instead competitive pricing every day. We have had clients who found cheaper hosting elsewhere and returned to us because the other service left a lot to be desired. We’ve seen dot-coms turn into dot-bombs, and there are many reasons why we’ve not only survived, but thrived.

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/why-should-i-choose-hosting-com#no-long-term-contracts)

No long-term contracts

We don’t believe in long-term contracts that prohibit you from making your own service decisions. Our hosting is month-to-month. If you’re not happy with our service for any reason, you are free to transfer your service to another provider at any time. That’s it! If you need a car, sign a contract. If you need a website, don’t.
