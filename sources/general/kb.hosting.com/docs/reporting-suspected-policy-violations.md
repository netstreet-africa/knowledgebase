# Source: https://kb.hosting.com/docs/reporting-suspected-policy-violations

This article describes how to report suspected policy violations to hosting.com. These policy violations may include, but are not limited to:

- Copyright infringement (DMCA violations).

 > 📘 Note To view our complete DMCA policy, please see [https://hosting.com/about/policies/dmca/](https://hosting.com/about/policies/dmca/).

- Spam violations.
- Other violations or abuse of our Acceptable Use Policy.

 > 📘 Note To view our complete Acceptable Use Policy (AUP), please visit [https://hosting.com/about/policies/](https://hosting.com/about/policies/).

In all of these cases, please send an email to _abuse(at)hosting.com_ (replace “(at)” with ”@”). For detailed information about what you should include in a request, please see the appropriate section of our policies page at [https://hosting.com/about/policies/](https://hosting.com/about/policies/).
