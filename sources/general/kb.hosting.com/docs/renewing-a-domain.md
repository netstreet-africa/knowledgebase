# Source: https://kb.hosting.com/docs/renewing-a-domain

This article describes how to use the Hosting Panel to manage renewals for domains you have purchased from hosting.com.

## 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/renewing-a-domain#configuring-automatic-renewal-for-a-domain)

Configuring automatic renewal for a domain

For greater convenience, you can configure your domains for automatic renewal. When automatic renewal is enabled, hosting.com automatically sends you a renewal invoice 14 days before the domain expires. This provides you with enough time to complete the renewal process before domain expiration. To configure automatic renewal for a domain, follow these steps:

1. Log in to the Hosting Panel at [https://my.hosting.com](https://my.hosting.com).
2. In the left sidebar, under **Products & Services**, click **Domains**: ![](https://files.readme.io/a5e49e2a4d9615d80b5d070b762b8365132a98dd3f67202407762f794a89728d-image.png)
3. On the **Domains** page, click the domain you want to configure for automatic renewal.
4. In the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
5. Scroll down the page to the **Renewal & Subscription Settings** section, and then click the **Auto-renew** slider to enable or disable automatic renewal: ![](https://files.readme.io/20000dca5346fc1e62b60e54ee0339eff9695608facb49c3cfcb10e9c4642c3e-image.png)

## 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/renewing-a-domain#renewing-a-domain)

Renewing a domain

To renew a domain that you previously purchased from hosting.com, follow these steps:

1. Log in to the Hosting Panel at [https://my.hosting.com](https://my.hosting.com).
2. In the left sidebar, under **Products & Services**, click **Domains**: ![](https://files.readme.io/a5e49e2a4d9615d80b5d070b762b8365132a98dd3f67202407762f794a89728d-image.png)
3. On the **Domains** page, click the domain you want to renew.

 > 👍 Tip You can filter the list of domains to only display active, canceled, or all domains. To do this, click the **Active**, **Cancelled**, **Pending Cancellation**, or **All** tab.

4. In the left sidebar, click **Billing**, and then pay the invoice for the domain renewal.
