# Source: https://kb.hosting.com/docs/do-you-comply-with-us-treasury-embargo-restrictions

Hosting.com complies with US Treasury embargo restrictions and sanctions.

## 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/do-you-comply-with-us-treasury-embargo-restrictions#countries-and-regions)

Countries and regions

We currently cannot provide any hosting services to individuals in the following countries and regions:

- [Cuba](http://www.treasury.gov/resource-center/sanctions/Programs/pages/cuba.aspx)
- [Iran](http://www.treasury.gov/resource-center/sanctions/Programs/pages/iran.aspx)
- [North Korea](http://www.treasury.gov/resource-center/sanctions/Programs/pages/nkorea.aspx)
- [Syria](http://www.treasury.gov/resource-center/sanctions/Programs/pages/syria.aspx)
- [Region of Crimea](http://www.treasury.gov/resource-center/sanctions/Programs/Pages/ukraine.aspx)

Treasury regulations also prohibit us from registering domains or hosting websites which use the TLDs associated with the countries and regions listed above. The specific prohibited TLDs are:

- .ir
- .kp
- .ss
- .sy
- .cu
- .crimea.ru

Additionally, we cannot provide services for .su (Soviet Union) domains.

## 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/do-you-comply-with-us-treasury-embargo-restrictions#specially-designated-nationals)

Specially Designated Nationals

In addition to the prohibited countries and regions listed above, we cannot do business with any SDNs (Specially Designated Nationals). To search the SDN sanctions list, please visit [https://www.treasury.gov/resource-center/sanctions/SDN-List/Pages/default.aspx](https://www.treasury.gov/resource-center/sanctions/SDN-List/Pages/default.aspx).
