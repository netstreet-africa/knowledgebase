This article discusses WHMCS license options and how to order a license.

## 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/ordering-a-whmcs-license#about-whmcs-licenses)

About WHMCS licenses

There are two types of WHMCS licenses available: branded and unbranded. The branded license has WHMCS branding on customer-facing pages. The unbranded license enables you to replace the WHMCS branding with branding for your own business. You must choose the type of license you want when you order.

The following WHMCS licenses are currently available:

- **Starter**: 250 clients, “Powered by WHMCS” branding.
- **Plus**: 250 clients, no branding.
- **Professional**: 500 clients, no branding.
- **Business 1000**: 1000 clients, no branding.
- **Business 2500**: 2500 clients, no branding.
- **Business 5000**: 5000 clients, no branding.
- **Business 10000**: 10000 clients, no branding.
- **Business Unlimited**: Unlimited clients, no branding.

## 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/ordering-a-whmcs-license#ordering-a-whmcs-license)

Ordering a WHMCS license

To order a WHMCS license for your account, please open a billing ticket on the Hosting Panel at [https://my.hosting.com](https://my.hosting.com). After the order is processed, you receive an e-mail message from hosting.com that contains the license key and instructions for [installing WHMCS](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/whmcs).
