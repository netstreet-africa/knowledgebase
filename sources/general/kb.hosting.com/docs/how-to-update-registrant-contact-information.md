Updating or changing registrant contact information

To change the registrant contact information for a domain, follow these steps:

1. Log in to the Hosting Panel at [https://my.hosting.com](https://my.hosting.com).
2. In the left sidebar, under **Products & Services**, click **Domains**:
3. On the Domains page, click the domain you want to configure.

Tip - You can filter the list of domains to only display active, canceled, or all domains. To do this, click the Active, Cancelled, or All tab.

4. Click **Update Registrant:**
5. In the Update Registrant dialog box, update the domain registrant contact information:
6. Click **Submit**.
