Hosting.com’s motto is “our speed, your success” — so what makes our hosting services fast? It is a combination of multiple technologies and best practices:

- [Turbo Servers](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/turbo-web-hosting)
- Solid State Drives (SSD)
- Servers fine-tuned by our support team
- Quadruple-redundant network
- [Cloudflare](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/cloudflare) content delivery network
- Choice of data center location
- 10 GB/s redundant network
- [HTTP/2](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/do-you-support-http-2), [SPDY](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/do-you-support-spdy) & ESI (Turbo option)
