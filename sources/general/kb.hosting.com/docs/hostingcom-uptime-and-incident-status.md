# Source: https://kb.hosting.com/docs/hostingcom-uptime-and-incident-status

Visit [https://status.hosting.com/](https://status.hosting.com/) to see uptime statistics for the past 30 days for:

- WordPress hosting.
- Domain and DNS services.
- Website and email hosting.
- Support and client infrastructure.

You can click the plus sign (**+**) icon next to any of these categories to view additional information about specific services. Additionally, visit [https://status.hosting.com/](https://status.hosting.com/) for the latest updates on any outages or incidents involving hosting.com services.
