# Source: https://kb.hosting.com/docs/expert-website-support-services-from-hostingcom

For site owners and web professionals who value their time, hosting.com offers expert, on-demand website support. Some of the tasks we can handle for you include:

- Malware removal and hacked site cleanup.
- Security protection and hardening.
- Error and bug fixing.
- WordPress core, theme, and plugin support.
- WordPress site migrations.
- Custom technical tasks on request.

These tasks include various fixes and optimizations that might have traditionally been handled by an in-house support team or by the site owner directly. Think of us as the expert sitting next to you, willing to take on all the tedious tasks you need resolved. For more information, or if you are ready to get started, please chat with us or open a support ticket at [https://my.hosting.com](https://my.hosting.com).

This is a best-effort service, and we commit to trying our absolute best. While some issues may not be fully resolvable, we always aim to provide practical workarounds or guidance.
