# Source: https://kb.hosting.com/docs/do-you-support-gdpr-compliance

Hosting.com supports the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This article provides information about hosting.com and GDPR. Hosting.com is committed to secure processing of data as well as transparency over any data collection practices. As part of GDPR compliance, hosting.com has updated its Privacy Policy. This policy update provides more clarity over our data processing practices and is designed to give you more control over your information. The policy is effective May 25th, 2018. You can read the policy at [https://hosting.com/about/policies/](https://hosting.com/about/policies/). Some customers may also need a Data Protection Agreement (DPA) as it is intended specifically to fulfill your own GDPR compliance. If you need the hosting.com DPA, please open a support ticket at [https://my.hosting.com](https://my.hosting.com). GDPR legislation aims to strengthen and unify data protection for all individuals within the European Union (EU). As a company that conducts business in the EU, hosting.com is required to comply with GDPR.

Hosting.com is already compliant with the US-EU Privacy Shield. For more information, please visit [https://hosting.com/about/policies/](https://hosting.com/about/policies/).
