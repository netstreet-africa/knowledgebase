# Source: https://kb.hosting.com/docs/using-the-ai-website-builder-for-wordpress

With the AI Website Builder for WordPress, powered by Extendify, you can quickly and easily:

- **Create a complete WordPress site**: With just a few clicks and text descriptions, AI Website Builder guides you through building a WordPress site from the ground up with an easy onboarding process.
- **Create, edit, and maintain site content**: The AI-enabled writing assistant includes several features to help you manage your content, while the image generator enables you to create high-quality images and logos based on text prompts.

## 

[​](https://kb.hosting.com/docs/using-the-ai-website-builder-for-wordpress#initial-site-onboarding)

Initial site onboarding

When you first log in to a new WordPress site, the AI Website Builder onboarding process starts automatically. During this process, you specify basic information about your site, select a page design, and AI Website Builder then creates a complete site. To do this, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your account at [https://my.hosting.com](https://my.hosting.com).
2. In the list of products and services, locate your Managed WordPress account, and then click **Login to Control Panel**.
3. When the control panel appears, in the left sidebar, click **Websites**: ![](https://files.readme.io/b2177385a849e7c2a46983574789490dedb0e4783be01bde68783a81b16d2333-image.png)
4. On the **Manage websites** page, click the website you want to manage.
5. In the **Applications installed** section, locate your WordPress site, and then click **Manage**: ![](https://files.readme.io/7f44bdd5a1e23d7b867af7b155bca07559aa9aee9e4c1a35a0d9156f8aab1110-image.png)
6. To log in to WordPress, click **Login to admin**: ![](https://files.readme.io/5691943c7fe05e0d5448a1b7547618dc3a80744842101fabbd0355107c4ddb04-image.png)
7. If this is your first time logging in to WordPress, the site onboarding process starts automatically. On the **What type of website are you creating?** page, select a website type and then click **Next**: ![](https://files.readme.io/d4fcfb1bedc6a0f4dbcf28762b0e089fbd4fb86bb568067d212c28cb4c2185f4-image.png)
8. On the **Tell Us About Your Website** page, complete the options and then click **Next**: ![](https://files.readme.io/3370b764d26426db22618edf7f5d3f5f62bf0060285414380d4da1a66785bdc8-image.png)
9. On the **Pick Your Site Structure** page, select one of the following options, and then click **Next**:
 - **Multi-Page Website**: Select this option if you are building a website with multiple pages.
 - **Single-Page Website**: Select this option if you are building a website that only has one page. ![](https://files.readme.io/630d248d3e5f5761d6e9f1a388fe808e88a1f62cca699efbb3eb93fce9d92e76-image.png)
10. On the **Pick a design for your website** page, select a design, and then click **Next**.
11. On the **Pick the pages to add to your website** page, select the check boxes next to the pages you want, and then click **Next**: ![](https://files.readme.io/cd2584a0897a1f137479b96fb84b339e73be54c06b9fe6d8f8f49281b3a0d4ea-image.png)
12. When AI Website Builder finishes building your site, click **View site** to view your new site! ![](https://files.readme.io/b89fca7442306484b041489f093bd9714e96329bd448cb24694f1f44d1860b72-image.png)

Creating and editing content

The AI Website Builder for WordPress includes numerous AI-enabled features to help you create and maintain site content, including:

- **Design Library:** You can choose from hundreds of page layouts, categorized by design type (for example, an “About” page, a blog post, a pricing page, etc).
- **AI Page Generator:** Describe the page you want to create, and Al generates a unique, ready-to-use page for you.
- **Content editing tools:** Use AI to fix spelling and grammatical errors, simplify language, make text shorter or longer, and more.
- **Image Generator:** Use AI to generate custom images based on a description.

To use these features, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your account at [https://my.hosting.com](https://my.hosting.com).
2. In the list of products and services, locate your Managed WordPress account, and then click **Login to Control Panel**.
3. When the control panel appears, in the left sidebar, click **Websites**: ![](https://files.readme.io/b2177385a849e7c2a46983574789490dedb0e4783be01bde68783a81b16d2333-image.png)
4. On the **Manage websites** page, click the website you want to manage.
5. In the **Applications installed** section, locate your WordPress site, and then click **Manage**: ![](https://files.readme.io/7f44bdd5a1e23d7b867af7b155bca07559aa9aee9e4c1a35a0d9156f8aab1110-image.png)
6. To log in to WordPress, click **Login to admin**: ![](https://files.readme.io/5691943c7fe05e0d5448a1b7547618dc3a80744842101fabbd0355107c4ddb04-image.png)
7. In the **Site Assistant** section of the WordPress administrative interface, click **Start Writing with AI**: ![](https://files.readme.io/651d0d665c506e073c33f004f2c95ab9d136c6deb6d3f62bb370c3582fb7de57-image.png)
8. The page editor appears: ![](https://files.readme.io/3fb9faaa6bd3e790c92e884001f6def9f0c94b789aceb39a28fb49798a0f3906-image.png)
 - To access the Design Library, click **Design Library**: ![](https://files.readme.io/091e83f1f4fa65bee8cc378534da78e3d224ff09723687a0383c13687de2225c-image.png) In the **Design Type** list box, you can filter the type of design you want to see. Click a design layout to use it: ![](https://files.readme.io/24e8345ea42cefaf889fab99a7b80db698b2d106c641922995cc84e80b18548a-image.png)
 - To generate page content using AI, click **AI Page Generator**: ![](https://files.readme.io/03012eef7d86c93adf340d72498b8bee3d4d0a35a3ad05a8690bcab583515cfc-image.png) Type a description for the page and site, and then click **Generate Page**.
 - To use the AI writing tools, select the text in the page you want to edit, and then in the **AI Tools** sidebar, click an option. You can generate new text, fix grammatical errors, and more: ![](https://files.readme.io/f0cab44f05091ad23d0ae95681a575e38690f21bafd6b9e92d61a6d01ba63a60-image.png)
 - To use the image generator, in the content editor type `/` and then click **Image**: ![](https://files.readme.io/0a865f4632a496d8e7e06a45cb07cc26d2c2ebdef2bb7176e0484adcd45bef0a-image.png) In the right sidebar, click **Generate an image**. In the **IMAGE DESCRIPTION** text box, type a description of the image you want to generate, and then click **Generate image**.
9. When you are ready to publish the page, in the top-right corner, click **Publish**: ![](https://files.readme.io/efcfd6075ad28b9093cac61bf2935869abaaeea40204782a5ffe9ee7d90f6fe5-image.png)
