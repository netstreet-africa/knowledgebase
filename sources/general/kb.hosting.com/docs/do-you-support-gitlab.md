# Source: https://kb.hosting.com/docs/do-you-support-gitlab

Yes, we do support GitLab on our unmanaged VPS hosting solutions. However, we do not support GitLab on our Shared Hosting accounts. GitLab has some complicated requirements that do not mesh well with a shared hosting environment.
