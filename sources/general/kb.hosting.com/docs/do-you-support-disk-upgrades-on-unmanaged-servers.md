# Source: https://kb.hosting.com/docs/do-you-support-disk-upgrades-on-unmanaged-servers

Yes, you can increase disk space for your unmanaged VPS. To do this, follow these steps:

1. Upgrade your existing plan to the new plan that you want.
2. Open a support ticket at [https://my.hosting.com](https://my.hosting.com) and specify when you want us to reboot the server so the new disk size can take effect.

- Data migration is not necessary when you upgrade disk space.
- You cannot downgrade (decrease) disk space for an unmanaged VPS.
