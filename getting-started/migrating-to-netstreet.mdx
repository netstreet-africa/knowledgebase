Moving your website to Netstreet Africa is straightforward whether your current host uses cPanel or not. This guide covers both the assisted cPanel account transfer process and the step-by-step manual migration path, and explains how to switch your DNS records once everything is in place. Taking a little extra care during the transition — particularly around timing — will keep downtime to an absolute minimum.

<Warning>
  We strongly recommend leaving **at least 7 days of overlap** between opening your Netstreet Africa account and closing your previous hosting account. This gives you time to test everything and weather any DNS propagation delays before committing to the switch.
</Warning>

## Before You Begin

Regardless of which migration method you use, keep these points in mind:

- **Use a separate email address.** When signing up with Netstreet Africa, use an email address that does *not* belong to the domain you are migrating. If that domain's email stops working mid-migration, you could miss important updates from our team.
- **Do not cancel your old account early.** Keep your previous hosting account active until you have fully tested your site on Netstreet Africa servers and confirmed that DNS has propagated.
- **Test before you switch.** You can preview your site on our servers using a temporary URL or a local `hosts` file before changing your name servers publicly.

## Option 1 — cPanel Account Transfer (Assisted Migration)

If your current hosting provider runs cPanel, our Migrations team can perform a full account transfer on your behalf. This includes your website files, databases, email accounts, and email data. The following account types are eligible:

- Shared hosting accounts
- Reseller hosting accounts
- Managed WordPress hosting accounts
- Managed VPS accounts
- Managed Dedicated Server accounts

<Note>
  If you are migrating WordPress sites to a Managed WordPress hosting account, your sites will appear automatically in WP Toolkit inside cPanel after the transfer is complete.
</Note>

### How to Request a cPanel Migration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for your Netstreet Africa account">
    If you have not already done so, create your account at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za). Remember to use an email address that is not hosted on the domain you are migrating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to the client area">
    Go to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a support ticket to the Migrations department">
    From the home page, click **Manage support tickets**, then click **Open Support Ticket**. Fill in the following details:

    - **Subject:** Site migration request
    - **Department:** Migrations
    - **Message:** Include the following information:
      - The destination product or service on your Netstreet Africa account
      - The domain name(s) to be migrated
      - Any SSL certificates you want transferred
      - cPanel login credentials, FTP details, or SSH connection details for your current host
      - Any special instructions or notes about your account
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation from our Migrations team">
    Our team processes migrations in the order they are received. We will keep you updated on progress. Migration speed can vary depending on your current host's network speed, the number of sites being migrated, and overall complexity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test your site on the Netstreet Africa server">
    Before changing your domain's name servers, test your site thoroughly using the temporary shared URL provided in the client area, or by adding a temporary entry to your local `hosts` file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your domain's name servers">
    Once you are satisfied with the migration, update your domain's name server settings to point to Netstreet Africa. If your domain is registered with us, you can do this from **Products & Services** in the client area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for DNS propagation">
    Allow up to **24–48 hours** for DNS changes to propagate fully across the internet, although many users will see the update much sooner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel your old hosting account">
    Only after you have confirmed your site is fully live and working on the Netstreet Africa servers should you cancel your account with the previous provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Option 2 — Manual Migration

If your current hosting provider does not use cPanel, or if you prefer to handle the migration yourself, you can move your data manually.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for your Netstreet Africa account">
    Create your account at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) if you have not done so already. Use an email address that is not on the domain being migrated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer your website files">
    Upload your website files to the `public_html` directory of your new Netstreet Africa hosting account. You can use FTP, SFTP, or the file manager in your control panel to do this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Migrate your email accounts">
    Re-create your email accounts in cPanel or your control panel, then migrate any existing email data across using an email client or the tools provided in your control panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export and import your databases">
    Export your MySQL or PostgreSQL databases from your old host (usually via phpMyAdmin or the command line), then import them into the database tool in your new control panel. Update your website's configuration files with the new database connection details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test your site before switching DNS">
    Use the shared temporary URL visible in the client area at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za), or modify your local `hosts` file, to verify the site looks and functions correctly on the Netstreet Africa server before going live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your domain's name servers">
    Point your domain to Netstreet Africa's name servers. If the domain is registered with Netstreet Africa, update the name servers from within the client area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for DNS propagation">
    DNS changes typically propagate within **24–48 hours**. During this window, some visitors may still be directed to your old host while others reach your new one — this is normal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel your old hosting account">
    Once the site is confirmed live on Netstreet Africa and DNS has fully propagated, you can safely close your account with the previous provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding DNS Propagation

DNS propagation is the time it takes for name server changes to spread across DNS resolvers worldwide. After you update your name servers:

- Most visitors will see your new site within **a few hours**
- Full propagation across all resolvers can take up to **48 hours**
- During propagation, different users in different locations may reach different servers — this is expected and temporary

<Info>
  You can check the current DNS status of your domain using a free tool like [dnschecker.org](https://dnschecker.org) to see how propagation is progressing in different regions.
</Info>

## Leaving Overlap Time

Running both your old and new hosting accounts simultaneously for at least a week is one of the most important steps in a successful migration. This overlap period gives you:

- Time to catch and fix any issues before your old account is gone
- A safe fallback if DNS propagation takes longer than expected
- The ability to compare the old and new environments side by side

<Tip>
  Mark your old account's cancellation date in your calendar for at least 7 days after your DNS change, and set a reminder to verify the migration is complete before that date arrives.
</Tip>
