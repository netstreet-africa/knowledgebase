The Netstreet Africa client area at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) is your central dashboard for managing all of your hosting products, domains, billing, and support tickets. This page walks you through creating an account, logging in for the first time, recovering a forgotten password, and signing out securely when you are done.

## Creating an Account

If you have not yet registered with Netstreet Africa, you need to create an account before you can log in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the client area">
    Open your browser and navigate to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create an account">
    On the Login page, click the **Create an account** link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the registration form">
    Fill in your name, email address, and a strong password, then click **Sign Up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Netstreet Africa. Click the **Verify Registration** link in that message to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Return to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and sign in with the email address and password you just created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Use an email address that you can reliably access. This address is used for billing notifications, renewal reminders, and support correspondence.
</Note>

## Logging In

Once your account is active, signing in takes just a few seconds.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the client area">
    Navigate to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    In the **Username** field, type the email address you registered with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your password">
    In the **Password** field, type your account password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Login">
    Click the **Login** button. The client area home page will load, showing a summary of your account and services.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resetting a Forgotten Password

If you have forgotten your password, you can request a reset link at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the login page">
    Navigate to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password?">
    Below the login form, click the **Forgot password?** link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type the email address associated with your Netstreet Africa account, then click **Send reset instructions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the email instructions">
    Check your inbox for a password reset email. Follow the link in the message to choose a new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you do not receive the reset email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. If it is still not there, make sure you are using the email address you registered with, or open a support ticket for assistance.
</Warning>

## Logging Out

Always log out when you have finished using the client area, particularly on shared or public computers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click your account icon">
    In the top-right corner of the client area, click the icon showing your initials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Logout">
    Select **Logout** from the dropdown menu. You will be returned to the login page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For extra account security, consider enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) from your profile settings. This adds a second verification step every time you log in.
</Tip>
