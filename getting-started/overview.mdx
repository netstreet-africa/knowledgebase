Welcome to Netstreet Africa! This guide walks you through the essentials you need to know after signing up for your hosting service or registering a domain. From logging in to the client area to managing your services and reaching our support team, everything you need to get started is covered below. If you ever have a question that is not answered here, our team is available around the clock and ready to help.

## What You Can Do in the Client Area

The Netstreet Africa client area at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) is your central hub for managing every aspect of your hosting account. From a single dashboard you can:

- View a summary of all your active products and services
- Manage domains, hosting packages, and email accounts
- Review and pay invoices, update payment methods, and change your billing cycle
- Open, view, and respond to support tickets
- Access your hosting control panel (cPanel or other) with a single click

## Logging In

To access your account, visit [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and enter the email address and password you used when you registered. For a full walkthrough — including how to reset a forgotten password — see the [Logging In](/getting-started/logging-in) guide.

## Managing Products and Services

All of your hosting packages, domain names, and email accounts appear under **Products & Services** in the client area. Click **Manage** next to any item to view its details, billing settings, and quick-access options. For more detail, see [Managing Products and Services](/getting-started/managing-products-and-services).

## Managing Domains

Inside **Products & Services** you can filter the list to show only your domain names. Clicking on a domain lets you:

- Lock or unlock the domain to prevent unauthorised transfers
- Update the domain's name servers to point to a different host
- Obtain an EPP/authorisation code for outbound transfers
- Toggle auto-renewal on or off

## Managing Billing

The **Billing** section of the client area shows your recent orders, outstanding invoices, and saved payment methods. Renewal invoices are generated automatically before your service renews, and payment is collected from your saved method. For a full explanation of how billing works, visit the [Billing FAQ](/account/billing-faq).

## Working with Support

Reaching the Netstreet Africa support team is straightforward. From the client area home page, click **Manage support tickets** to create a new ticket or check the status of an existing one. You can also use the live-chat button at the bottom right of the screen for real-time help.

<Tip>
  Before opening a ticket, check the Help Centre — many common questions are answered here and you may find a faster solution on your own.
</Tip>
