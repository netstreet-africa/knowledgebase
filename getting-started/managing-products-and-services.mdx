After logging in to the Netstreet Africa client area at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za), the **Products & Services** section gives you a complete view of everything you have with us. From here you can manage hosting packages, domain names, and email accounts — and jump directly into your control panel with a single click.

## Viewing Your Products and Services

To see all your active products and services, log in to the client area and click **Products & Services** in the main navigation. The page displays a list of every item on your account, including hosting plans, domain registrations, and email services.

<Info>
  Each item in the list shows its current status (active, suspended, pending, etc.), the renewal date, and a quick **Manage** button for easy access.
</Info>

## Managing an Individual Product or Service

Clicking the **Manage** button next to any product opens a detail page with everything you need to configure and monitor that service. Depending on what you select, you will see:

- **Overview** — A summary of the product, including server details, disk usage, and bandwidth (for hosting packages) or registration and expiry dates (for domains).
- **Billing** — The renewal date, billing cycle, and payment method for that specific service. You can update the payment method or generate a renewal invoice from here.
- **Settings** — Additional options such as adding account delegates, adjusting login settings, or enabling specific features.

### Accessing Your Control Panel

For hosting products, the **Manage** page includes a **Login to Control Panel** button that signs you in to cPanel or your assigned control panel automatically — no separate username or password required.

<Tip>
  If you have multiple hosting packages, make sure you click **Manage** on the correct package before clicking **Login to Control Panel**, especially if your packages are on different servers.
</Tip>

## Managing Your Domains

Inside the **Products & Services** section, you can filter the list to show only domain names. Clicking on a domain gives you access to these management options:

| Action | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Lock / Unlock domain** | Prevents or allows outbound transfers to another registrar. Domains should be locked unless you are actively transferring. |
| **Update name servers** | Points your domain to a different hosting provider's DNS servers. |
| **Get EPP code** | Retrieves the authorisation code needed to transfer your domain away from Netstreet Africa. |
| **Auto-renewal settings** | Toggles automatic renewal on or off for the domain. |

<Warning>
  If you disable auto-renewal on a domain, you are responsible for renewing it manually before it expires. Expired domains can be re-registered by anyone, so set a reminder well in advance of the expiry date.
</Warning>

## Managing Billing for a Service

Each product or service has its own **Billing** tab. From there you can:

- See the next renewal date and the amount due
- Switch the payment method linked to that specific service
- Click **Issue next invoice** to generate a renewal invoice early if you want to pay ahead of time

For general billing information — including how automatic renewals work, what happens when payment is late, and refund policies — see the [Billing FAQ](/account/billing-faq).

## Cancelling a Product or Service

If you no longer need a service, you can cancel it from the **Manage** page. Cancelling schedules the service to expire at the end of the current billing term, preventing any further automatic charges. If you change your mind before the term ends, contact our support team and we can reactivate the service.

<Note>
  Cancelling a hosting package does not automatically cancel associated domain names, and vice versa. Make sure you cancel each product individually if you wish to end all services.
</Note>
