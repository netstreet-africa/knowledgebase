Movilo brings your gym’s daily work into one place. Use this guide to find the right starting point for your role.

## What Movilo is

Movilo runs a gym’s day: members and their memberships, invoices and payments, classes and bookings, check-in at the desk or through a gate, signed contracts, enquiries from the gym’s website, and the gym’s own branded member portal.

Many gyms share one Movilo. Each gym’s records are kept apart from every other gym’s, and staff only ever see the gyms they belong to.

## Who does what

| Person | What they do | Where they work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Movilo operator** | Runs the platform for Netstreet: sets plans and prices, bills gyms for their subscription, and helps gyms that are stuck. | The operator console, at `/platform` on Movilo’s main address |
| **Gym owner** | Sets up the gym, its team, prices, contract, branding and subscription. | The workspace, at Movilo’s address or the gym’s own |
| **Gym staff** | Branch managers, finance, reception and coaches. Each role sees only what its work needs. | The workspace |
| **Member** | Joins, signs their contract, books classes and comes through the door. | The member portal, the join page and the signing page |
