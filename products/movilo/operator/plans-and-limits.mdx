Configure Movilo subscription prices and limits for members, branches, teams and gates, including custom-domain and white-label features.

Each plan sets how many active members, branches, team members and gates a gym may have. Use −1 for unlimited and 0 for none. Two switches add features: **white label**, which hides Movilo and Netstreet from the gym’s members and staff, and **the gym’s own domain**.

The starting plans are placeholders. Set the real prices before anyone sees the pricing page.

| Starting plan | Members | Branches | Team | Gates | Own domain | White label |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter, R799 a month | 150 | 1 | 3 | 1 | No | No |
| Growth, R1 499 a month | 500 | 3 | 10 | 4 | Yes | No |
| Scale, R2 999 a month | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Yes | Yes |

A gym can’t move to a plan its current use doesn’t fit. If a plan loses white label or own domain, gyms on it lose that feature straight away, and get it back if they upgrade.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
