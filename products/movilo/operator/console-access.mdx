Set up an authorised Netstreet operator account and open the Movilo platform console on the main address to manage the service.

1. Sign up on Movilo’s main address with your work email, and confirm it from the email Movilo sends.
2. Someone with access to the server runs this once, with your address:

   ```bash
   npm run platform:admin -- you@netstreet.co.za --remote
   ```
3. Sign in again and open **/platform**. Your account also shows a link to it under Settings, Your account.

<Note>
**The console only works on Movilo’s main address.** On a gym’s own address or subdomain, operator powers are switched off, even for your account. That protects you if a gym’s domain is ever misused.
</Note>

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
