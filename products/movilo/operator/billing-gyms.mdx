Issue subscription invoices, confirm EFT payments, handle renewals and understand trial, active, overdue and read-only gym statuses.

Gyms pay Movilo by EFT against an invoice. Nothing is charged automatically.

1. **Trial.** A new gym gets 14 days free. You can change the end date under Gyms, Manage.
2. **Choosing a plan.** The owner picks a plan under Settings, Subscription. Movilo issues an invoice and emails it, with Netstreet’s bank details and the invoice number as the payment reference.
3. **Confirming payment.** When the money shows on the bank statement, find the invoice under Invoices and choose **Mark paid**, entering the bank reference. The gym becomes active until the end of the period the invoice covers.
4. **Renewals.** Press **Issue due renewals** regularly, for example every Monday. It isn’t automatic yet.

| Gym status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Free trial | Everything works until the trial ends. |
| Active | Paid up. |
| Payment due | The paid period ended less than seven days ago. Everything still works, and the owner sees a reminder. |
| Expired | The trial ended, or payment is more than seven days late. The gym can read everything but can’t make changes until it pays. |
| Suspended or cancelled | Set by you. Read-only, with data kept. |

<Note>
A read-only gym’s gates keep working, so its members aren’t locked out because the gym is late paying Movilo.
</Note>

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
