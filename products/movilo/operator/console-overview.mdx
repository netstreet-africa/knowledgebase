Explore Movilo’s operator tools for gyms, subscriptions, invoices, plans, email and activity, including helping a gym owner regain access.

| Tab | What it’s for |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Paying gyms, gyms on trial, gyms that need attention because payment is due or their trial ended, suspended gyms, total members, monthly revenue and open invoices. |
| **Gyms** | Every gym with its owner, status, plan, address, members, branches, team and gates. **Manage** opens a gym: change its plan, set its trial end date, add an owner by email, issue a subscription invoice, or suspend and reactivate it. |
| **Invoices** | Subscription invoices to gyms. View, email, mark paid or void each one. **Issue due renewals** invoices every active gym whose paid period ends within seven days. |
| **Plans** | Movilo’s plans: price, member, branch, team and gate limits, whether they include white label and the gym’s own domain, and whether they show on the pricing page. |
| **Email** | How Movilo itself sends email, a check of the sending domain, and Movilo’s email wording. |
| **Invoice design** | Netstreet’s details on subscription invoices: legal name, registration and VAT numbers, address, bank details for EFT, numbering and due days. |
| **Website** | Code to show Movilo’s plans and a sign-up button on Netstreet’s website. |
| **Sent emails** | Every email Movilo sent or tried to send, with failures. When email isn’t set up, messages wait here instead. |
| **Activity** | A permanent log of what operators did in the console. It can’t be changed or deleted. |

## Helping a gym owner who lost access

Ask them to sign up again with an email they can reach. Then open the gym under Gyms, choose Manage, and use **Add an owner by email**. They become an owner alongside anyone already there.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
