Send a join request through your gym’s website or join link, choose a membership, add your identification and photo, or join at reception.

If your gym’s website has a Join button, or they sent you a join link:

1. Choose your plan.
2. Enter your name, email and, if you like, your phone number.
3. Enter your South African ID number, or your passport number and the country that issued it. It goes on your membership contract and is stored encrypted.
4. Add a photo of your face, with your phone’s camera or an upload. The gym’s team sees it when you arrive, to check it’s you.
5. Agree that the gym can contact you, and send your request. The gym will be in touch.

You can also join at the gym’s front desk, where staff add you and take your photo.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
