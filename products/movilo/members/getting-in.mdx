Use reception, a QR pass, a card, a passcode or enrolled biometrics to enter your gym, and find out what to check when access is blocked.

Depending on your gym, you come in by:

- **Checking in at reception**, or showing your **check-in pass**.
- **A card** at the gate, once reception has added it.
- **A passcode** at the gate: type your gate ID, then your code. Ask reception for your gate ID.
- **Your fingerprint or face** at the gate, once you’ve agreed and been enrolled at the gym. You can withdraw your consent at any time, and the gym then deletes your templates.

## If you can’t get in

It’s usually one of these: your membership is frozen or ended, your contract isn’t signed yet, or an invoice is overdue. Speak to reception. Once it’s sorted, the gates know within seconds.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
