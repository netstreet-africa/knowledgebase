Book and cancel classes, check your account and balance, find your contract and open a reception check-in pass in your gym’s member portal.

| Tab | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Classes** | See upcoming classes and book a spot. If a class is full, you join the waitlist and move up when a space opens. |
| **My bookings** | See and cancel your bookings. Cancelling gives your class credit back. |
| **My account** | Your details, photo, ID (shown as its last digits), contract and balance. Ask your gym to change anything here. |
| **Check-in pass** | A QR code for the front desk. It works once, within two minutes, so open it when you arrive. |

If your gym doesn’t have your photo yet, the portal asks you to add one. You can add it once. After that, only the gym can change it.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
