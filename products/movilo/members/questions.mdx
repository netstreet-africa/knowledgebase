Find out who can see your member information, how to pay your gym and what to check when a Movilo email does not reach your inbox.

## Who can see my information?

Your gym’s team, and only as much as each person’s role needs. Your full ID number is hidden from most staff, and viewing it is recorded. Netstreet runs Movilo for your gym, and your gym decides how your information is used.

## How do I pay?

Your gym emails your invoices with a link to view them. Pay the way your gym asks, usually EFT or at the desk. Your balance shows under My account.

## I didn’t get an email

Check your spam folder, and that your gym has the right email address. You can ask for a new confirmation email after signing in.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
