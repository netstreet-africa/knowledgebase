Create your Movilo account using the email your gym has for you, confirm your email address and reset your password when you need to.

- **Create an account** with the same email address your gym has for you, and a password of at least 10 characters.
- **Confirm your email** from the message you receive. Until you do, you can’t open your membership.
- **Forgot your password?** Choose “Forgot password” on the sign-in page. The link lasts an hour.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
