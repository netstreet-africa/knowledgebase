Open your gym’s contract, check your details, add your signature and confirm your signing code, then find your signed copy in Movilo.

If your gym uses online contracts, you’ll get an email asking you to sign. You can’t check in or book until you do.

1. Open the link in the email. It lasts 30 days. You can also choose **Read and sign** in your member portal.
2. Read the contract, and check your details at the top are right.
3. Tick each statement to confirm it.
4. Sign in the box with your finger or mouse, or choose Type to use your name.
5. Confirm that you’re signing electronically. Your electronic signature is as binding as one on paper.
6. Ask for a code. It’s texted to your mobile, or emailed if your gym can’t text you. Enter it within 10 minutes and choose **Sign contract**.

You’ll get an email with a link to your signed copy, which is also in your member portal. If you signed online, you can cancel without reason within 7 days by telling your gym in writing.

<Warning>
If you get a signing code you didn’t ask for, don’t share it, and tell your gym. Someone may have your signing link.
</Warning>

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
