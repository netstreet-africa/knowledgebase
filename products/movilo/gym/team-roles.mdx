Understand the owner, branch manager, finance, reception and coach roles in Movilo, then invite your team and assign their branches.

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including prices, team, branding, contract, gates and the subscription. Every gym needs at least one owner. |
| **Branch manager** | Day-to-day work: members, schedules, bookings, check-in, payments received, enquiries, reports and gates. Can be tied to one branch. |
| **Finance** | Invoices, payments, arrears and reports. |
| **Reception** | Members, payments received, bookings and check-in, and members’ cards and passcodes. |
| **Coach** | Classes and check-in. Sees no contact details, ID numbers or money. |

Invite someone under **Settings, Team**. They get an email with a link that lasts seven days, and must sign in with the same email address. Staff tied to one branch only work in that branch.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
