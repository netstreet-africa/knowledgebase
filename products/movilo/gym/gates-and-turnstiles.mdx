Connect compatible ZKTeco ADMS terminals to Movilo, sync member access, manage cards and biometrics, and look after your gym’s gates.

Movilo works with ZKTeco terminals that have the ADMS cloud setting, such as SpeedFace models. The terminal connects to Movilo over the internet by itself, so there’s nothing to install at the gym, and no ports to open.

## Connecting a gate

1. Under **Gates**, choose Add a gate. Enter a name, the terminal’s serial number (on its label, or under System info on the terminal), the branch, and whether it’s for entry or exit.
2. Movilo shows the gate’s own server address, such as `gate-7wvrybk2zx.movilo.co.za`.
3. On the terminal, open **Menu, COMM., Cloud Server Setting**. Some models call it ADMS. Set Server mode to ADMS, turn on **Enable domain name**, enter the server address and port **443**, and turn on **HTTPS**. Leave the proxy off.
4. **Give the terminal an administrator first**, using an ID from 1 to 99. Movilo never touches those IDs, and a terminal without an administrator lets anyone open its menu.
5. Within a minute the gate shows as online, and allowed members start arriving on it.

## How it works

- Members who may come in are sent to every gate within about ten seconds of any change. When someone freezes, cancels or falls into arrears, they’re removed just as quickly.
- Members get gate IDs from 1001. Change one on the member’s profile to match an older system if you need to.
- A card or passcode enrolled at one gate reaches all your others.
- **Fingerprints and faces** are only shared between gates once you’ve recorded the member’s consent on their profile. Record consent first, then enrol them at any gate under their gate ID. Without consent, Movilo keeps nothing and the member’s profile tells you.
- Entries become check-ins at the gate’s branch. Exit gates record leaving only.

## Looking after gates

- **Recent entries** on the Gates page lists every entry. **Remove from gate** deletes an ID Movilo doesn’t know from the terminal.
- **Sync everyone again** resends all your members to a gate. **Refresh details** asks for its model and counts. **Restart** restarts it.
- **New address** gives the gate a new server address, which you then enter on the terminal. Use it if the old address may have been seen by someone it shouldn’t.
- A warning shows if a gate connects without HTTPS. Turn HTTPS on in its settings if the terminal offers it.

<Note>
Remote door opening isn’t available yet. Terminals that need ZKTeco’s separate access-panel software, such as InBio and C3 controllers, aren’t supported.
</Note>
