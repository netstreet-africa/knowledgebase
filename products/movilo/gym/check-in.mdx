Use reception check-in, scan a member’s QR pass and monitor gate arrivals in Movilo, including photo checks and unexpected-entry warnings.

## At the door

The top of the **Check-in** page shows whoever just came in, with their photo, the time and how they got in, and refreshes every few seconds. Look up and check the face in front of you matches. Red warnings appear when a gate let in someone who isn’t allowed, or someone used an ID Movilo doesn’t know.

## Ways to check someone in

- **At reception:** search for the member and choose Check in.
- **With a QR pass:** the member opens a pass in their portal. Scan it into the field on the Check-in page, or paste its code. A pass works once, within two minutes.
- **Through a gate:** see below. Entries become check-ins by themselves.

The same person can’t be checked in twice within two minutes.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
