Issue and email member invoices, record EFT or cash payments, manage arrears and export your gym’s billing records from Movilo.

- **Issue an invoice** under Billing, or from a member’s profile. Tick **Email this invoice** to send it at once.
- **Emailed invoices** carry a private link that works for 90 days. Sending again replaces the link. If email isn’t set up, Movilo shows you the link to share yourself.
- **Record a payment** when money arrives by EFT or cash. Movilo records it; it doesn’t collect money or check your bank.
- **Arrears.** Overdue invoices show in red. Once one is past your grace period, the member can’t check in, book or come through a gate until it’s paid.
- **Export** invoices to a spreadsheet with the Export button.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
