Set up membership prices, billing periods and class credits in Movilo. Learn how monthly memberships and once-off class packs work.

Under **Memberships**, create each plan with a name, price, billing period (monthly or a once-off pack), description and class credits. Use −1 for unlimited classes. Creating a monthly plan doesn’t charge anyone automatically: you invoice members yourself.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
