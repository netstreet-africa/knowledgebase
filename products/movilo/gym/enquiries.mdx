Track website enquiries and walk-ins in Movilo, move them through follow-up stages and convert them to members with their details intact.

People who ask to join on your website arrive under **Enquiries**, with the plan they chose, their photo and their ID number. You can also add walk-ins yourself. Move each one through New, Contacted and Trial visit. **Convert to member** creates their member profile, carries their photo and ID number across, and sends your contract if you have one.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
