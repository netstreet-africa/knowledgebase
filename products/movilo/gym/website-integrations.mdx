Add membership pricing to your website, share plan-specific join links and manage join requests through Movilo’s website integrations.

Under **Integrations**:

- **Pricing on your site.** Copy the website code into your site. It works with WordPress (a Custom HTML block), Wix (Embed HTML), Squarespace (a Code block), Webflow (an Embed element) and most others. Choose a theme, button colour and button text first. Prices update by themselves within a minute of a change.
- **Iframe.** Use this if your website builder doesn’t allow scripts.
- **Order links.** Each plan has its own join link to use on buttons, emails and social posts.
- **Join requests.** Turn them on or off, and choose which plans appear on your website. People who join give their details, ID or passport number and a photo, and you’re emailed about each new request.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
