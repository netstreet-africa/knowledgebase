Add members to Movilo with their photo and identification, manage their profiles and memberships, and check who is allowed to enter.

## Adding a member

Choose **Add member**. You need:

- **Their photo.** Take it with the desk’s camera, or upload one. Staff see it every time the member checks in or comes through a gate, to make sure it’s them.
- **Their South African ID number, or passport number and issuing country.** An ID number is checked as you type, and shows the date of birth it holds, so typing mistakes are caught. It goes on their contract. Two members of your gym can’t share a number.
- Their name, email and phone, and optionally their membership and home branch.

If you’ve published a contract, Movilo emails it to them to sign. If you haven’t, tick the box to record that they accepted your contract.

## A member’s profile

Select a member to open their profile. From there you can:

- Check them in, issue an invoice, freeze or reactivate their membership, or cancel it.
- Assign a membership. This resets their class credits to the new plan’s allowance.
- Change their photo, or add one if they don’t have one yet.
- See their ID as its last four digits. **Show number** reveals the whole number, and each time is recorded in your activity.
- See their contract, send it again, or open it for them to sign at the desk.
- Set their gate ID, card number and passcode, and record consent for fingerprints and faces.
- Preview their member portal as they see it.

## Who may come in

A member can check in, book classes and come through a gate only when all of these are true:

- their membership is active, not frozen or cancelled;
- they have a membership plan;
- their contract is signed or accepted;
- no invoice is overdue beyond your grace period, which is three days unless you change it under Settings, General.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
