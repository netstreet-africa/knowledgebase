Find the Movilo settings for your gym’s region, subscription, team, branches, branding, domain, contracts, email and invoice details.

| Tab | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Gym name and city, and your region: country, timezone, currency and arrears grace period. Also your own password. |
| **Subscription** | Your Movilo plan, how much of it you use, and invoices to pay. Pay by EFT using the invoice number as the reference. Your plan activates once Netstreet confirms payment. |
| **Team** | Invite people, change their role or branch, or remove them. |
| **Branches** | Add, close or reopen branches. |
| **Branding** | Logo, brand colour and member portal address. On plans that include it, **white label** hides Movilo and Netstreet from your members and team, and lets you link your own terms and privacy policy. |
| **Domain** | Your Movilo address, such as `yourgym.movilo.co.za`, which works straight away. On plans that include it, connect **your own domain**, such as `members.yourgym.co.za`: add the TXT and CNAME records Movilo shows at your DNS provider, then press Check now until it’s live. A subdomain works best, and your main website can stay where it is. |
| **Contracts** | Your membership contract. See [Contracts](/products/movilo/gym/contracts). |
| **Email** | Send from your own mail server (with presets for Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Resend and others), check your domain, change the wording of your emails, send tests, and see what was sent. |
| **Invoices** | Your legal name, registration and VAT numbers, address, contact details, bank details, invoice numbering, due days, layout and notes. With a VAT number and rate, invoices read “Tax invoice” and show the VAT included. |

<Note>
On your own domain or Movilo subdomain, everyone who signs in only sees your gym. Password reset and email confirmation links always use your Movilo address, never your own domain.
</Note>

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
