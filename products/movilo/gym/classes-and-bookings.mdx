Add classes with coaches, rooms and capacity limits in Movilo, manage bookings and waitlists, and understand how class credits work.

- **Add a class** under Schedule: name, coach, room, branch, date and time in your gym’s time zone, length, capacity and category. Movilo stops a room or a coach being double-booked.
- **Booking.** Members book in their portal, or staff book them. A full class puts people on a waitlist, and they move up automatically when someone cancels.
- **Credits.** A confirmed booking uses one class credit, and cancelling gives it back. Bookings close once a class starts.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
