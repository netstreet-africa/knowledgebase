Write and publish your gym’s membership contract in Movilo, send signing links, collect signatures and manage new contract versions.

## Writing your contract

Under **Settings, Contracts**, edit the title, fixed term (up to 24 months, or 0 for month to month), the sections, and the statements a member must tick. Movilo starts you with plain example wording. **Replace it with your own and have a lawyer check it.**

These fields fill in each member’s details:

| Field | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `{{member_name}}`, `{{member_email}}`, `{{member_phone}}` | The member’s name, email and phone |
| `{{member_id_number}}`, `{{member_birth_date}}` | Their ID or passport number, and date of birth |
| `{{gym}}`, `{{gym_legal_name}}`, `{{gym_registration}}`, `{{gym_address}}` | Your gym name and business details from Settings, Invoices |
| `{{plan}}`, `{{fee}}` | Their membership and its price |
| `{{start_date}}`, `{{term_months}}`, `{{end_date}}` | When it starts, the fixed term, and when the term ends |

**Preview with sample details** shows how it reads. **Publish** makes it live.

## What happens once it’s published

- Every new member is emailed a private link to sign, which lasts 30 days. They can’t check in, book or use a gate until they sign. The old tick box for contract acceptance disappears.
- They read it, tick each statement, sign by drawing or typing their name, and confirm a six-digit code. The code is texted to their mobile if Movilo can send texts and their number works, or emailed if not.
- Movilo keeps a signed PDF with a final page showing how it was signed: the time, internet address, device, where the code went, and a fingerprint of exactly what they saw. Nobody can change or delete a signed contract, including you.
- Members who signed online can cancel without reason within 7 days.

## Changing your contract

Publish a new version. New members sign it. Members who signed earlier keep the version they signed. To ask someone to sign the new version, use **Email again** on their profile. They keep access while it waits.

## Signing at the desk

**Sign here** on a member’s profile opens their contract on this device, such as a tablet at reception. They still confirm the code sent to their own phone or email.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
