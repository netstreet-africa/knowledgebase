Understand how Movilo handles member photos, identification numbers, biometric consent and signed contracts within your gym workspace.

Your gym is responsible for your members’ information under POPIA. Movilo helps by keeping it tight:

- **Encrypted:** ID and passport numbers, gate passcodes, fingerprint and face templates, and mail passwords.
- **Photos** open only for your staff and the member themself.
- **ID numbers** show as their last four digits. Viewing a whole number is recorded. Coaches see none.
- **Biometrics** need recorded consent, and are deleted when consent is withdrawn or the member is cancelled.
- **Signed contracts** hold the full ID number, as a paper contract would, so treat them as sensitive.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
