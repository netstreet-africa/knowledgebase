Review membership health, class capacity, enquiry conversion and workspace activity in Movilo, then export your gym’s records.

Under **Reports**: membership health (active, frozen and cancelled), class capacity used, enquiries turned into members, and your workspace activity, a record of every change your team made. Export members, attendance and enquiries to spreadsheets.

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
