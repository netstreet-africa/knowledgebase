Set up your gym, add your branding and business details, create memberships, publish your contract and invite your team to Movilo.

1. **Create your account.** Sign up with your name, email and a password of at least 10 characters, and confirm your email from the message Movilo sends.
2. **Create your gym.** Enter its name, city and country. The country sets your timezone and currency, which you can change later. Your 14-day free trial starts now. You can also explore two sample gyms first, filled with made-up data.
3. **Add your branding** under Settings, Branding: logo, colour and the address of your member portal.
4. **Fill in your business details** under Settings, Invoices. Your contract and invoices use your legal name, registration number and address.
5. **Create your memberships** under Memberships.
6. **Write and publish your contract** under Settings, Contracts, if you want new members to sign one.
7. **Invite your team** under Settings, Team.
8. **Add your members.**

<Tip>
**Running more than one location?** Add each as a branch under Settings, Branches, rather than as a separate gym. Members can visit any branch.
</Tip>

<Note>Based on the Movilo v0.1.0 guide. Available features depend on your role, your plan and your gym’s configuration.</Note>
