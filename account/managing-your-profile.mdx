Your profile in the Netstreet client area holds the personal and contact information associated with your account. Keeping this information current is important — it ensures you receive renewal reminders, billing notifications, and support correspondence at the right address, and that our team can verify your identity when you contact us.

## Accessing Your Profile Settings

To view and edit your profile, log in to the client area at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za). Click the icon showing your initials in the top-right corner, then select **Profile** or **Account Settings** from the dropdown menu.

## Updating Your Contact Information

From the **Profile** page you can update:

- **First and last name** — The name on your account and correspondence
- **Company name** — Optional; useful if you manage hosting on behalf of a business
- **Phone number** — Used for account verification and urgent contact
- **Physical and postal address** — Required for billing and domain WHOIS records

After making changes, click **Save** to apply the updates.

<Note>
  If you have domains registered through Netstreet, keep in mind that changes to your profile contact details may need to be updated separately in your domain's WHOIS record. Contact support if you need assistance with that.
</Note>

## Changing Your Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your profile settings">
    Log in to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and navigate to your account profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the password section">
    Find the **Change Password** or **Security** section of the profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current and new passwords">
    Type your current password to confirm your identity, then enter and confirm your new password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** or **Update Password** to apply the new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a password that is at least 12 characters long and includes a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid reusing passwords from other services.
</Tip>

## Updating Your Default Email Address

Your default email address is the one used for all billing and system notifications. To change it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your profile settings">
    Log in to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and open your profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the email field">
    Replace your current email address with the new one you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the new address">
    A verification email will be sent to the new address. Click the link in that email to confirm the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you are changing your email address to one hosted on a domain you manage through Netstreet, be aware that any email disruption (such as during a migration) could prevent you from receiving verification messages. Use an independent email provider such as Gmail or Outlook if possible.
</Warning>

## Viewing Your Support PIN

Your support PIN is a short numeric code that verifies your identity when you contact Netstreet by phone or live chat. It prevents anyone else from making changes to your account without authorisation.

To find your support PIN:

1. Log in to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za).
2. Navigate to your profile or account settings page.
3. Locate the **Support PIN** section. The PIN will be displayed there.

<Info>
  Keep your support PIN private. Never share it with anyone other than a Netstreet support agent during a verified support interaction.
</Info>

## Managing Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security to your login by requiring a one-time code in addition to your password. To enable 2FA:

1. Log in to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and go to your profile settings.
2. Find the **Two-Factor Authentication** or **Security** section.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to link an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Authy) to your account.
4. Save your backup codes in a secure location in case you lose access to your authenticator device.

<Warning>
  If you lose access to your authenticator app and your backup codes, recovering your account will require identity verification with the support team. Store your backup codes somewhere safe and accessible.
</Warning>
