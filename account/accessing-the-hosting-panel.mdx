The Netstreet Africa client area at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) is your gateway to managing all your hosting products and services. From here you can access your control panel, manage domains, view invoices, and open support tickets. This article explains how to create an account, sign in, recover a forgotten password, and log out safely.

## Signing Up for an Account

If you do not already have a Netstreet Africa account, you need to create one before you can log in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the client area">
    Open your web browser and go to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create an account">
    On the **Login** page, click the **Create an account** link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the registration form">
    Fill in your full name, email address, and a secure password, then click **Sign Up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    You will receive an email at the address you provided. Open it and click **Verify Registration** to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Return to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and sign in using the email address and password you created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Choose an email address you actively monitor — it will receive renewal reminders, billing notifications, and responses to your support tickets.
</Note>

## Logging In to the Client Area

Once your account is active, follow these steps to sign in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the client area">
    Navigate to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    In the **Username** text box, type the email address you used when you created your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your password">
    In the **Password** text box, type your account password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Login">
    Click the **Login** button. The client area home page will load, displaying an overview of your account and a summary of your services.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resetting Your Password

If you forget your password, you can request a reset link at any time without contacting support.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the login page">
    Navigate to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password?">
    Below the login form, click the **Forgot password?** link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    In the **Email** text box, type the email address associated with your account, then click **Send reset instructions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the reset instructions">
    Open the email from Netstreet Africa and follow the link to set a new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If the reset email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk mail folder. If you are still unable to log in, open a support ticket from the Netstreet Africa website and our team will assist you.
</Warning>

## Logging Out of the Client Area

Always log out when you have finished your session, especially on shared or public computers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click your account icon">
    In the top-right corner of the client area, click the icon that displays your initials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Logout">
    Click **Logout** from the dropdown menu. You will be redirected to the login page, confirming your session has ended.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For stronger account security, enable two-factor authentication (2FA) from your profile settings. With 2FA active, anyone trying to log in will need both your password and a one-time code from your authenticator app.
</Tip>
