The Netstreet support team is available 24/7/365 and is always ready to help. The fastest way to get help with a technical issue or account question is by opening a support ticket through the client area at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za). This article explains how to open a ticket, what to include in your message, and how to review your past tickets.

## Opening a Support Ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the client area">
    Go to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and sign in with your email address and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Manage support tickets">
    On the home page dashboard, click the **Manage support tickets** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Support page">
    The **Support** page loads and displays any tickets you have already submitted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Open Support Ticket">
    Click the **Open Support Ticket** button to start a new ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the ticket form">
    Fill in the following fields in the **Submit a support ticket** form:

    - **Subject** — A short, clear description of the issue (e.g. "Website returning 500 error after plugin update")
    - **Department** — Select the team best placed to help (Technical Support, Billing, Migrations, Sales, etc.)
    - **Message** — Describe your issue in as much detail as possible. See the [Writing a Helpful Ticket](#writing-a-helpful-ticket) section below for guidance.
    - **Attachments** (optional) — You can attach files in **PDF**, **JPG**, **PNG**, or **DOCX** format to support your request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit ticket">
    Click **Submit ticket** to send your request. The Netstreet support team will respond as quickly as possible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  After submitting your ticket, you will receive an email confirmation with a ticket reference number. You can use this number to find your ticket in the Support page at any time.
</Note>

## Writing a Helpful Ticket

A well-written ticket gets you a faster, more accurate response. The more context you provide, the less back-and-forth is needed before we can resolve your issue.

### Before You Write

<Tip>
  Check the [Netstreet Help Centre](https://my.netstreet.co.za) before opening a ticket. Many common problems have documented solutions, and you may be able to resolve the issue yourself without waiting for a response.
</Tip>

### What to Include

Use the checklist below as a guide. Not every item applies to every situation, but include anything that is relevant.

<Accordion title="What have you already tried?">
  Describe any troubleshooting steps you have already taken and what the results were. This prevents the support team from suggesting things you have already done and helps narrow down the cause faster.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the exact error message?">
  If an error message appears on your screen or in a log file, copy and paste the exact text into your ticket. Paraphrasing an error message can make it much harder to identify the underlying cause.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is your environment?">
  Include details about your setup, such as:
  - Your operating system (e.g. Windows 11, macOS Sonoma, Ubuntu 22.04)
  - Your browser and version (e.g. Chrome 124, Firefox 126)
  - Your CMS and version (e.g. WordPress 6.5, Joomla 5.1)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What changed recently?">
  Think about any recent changes that might be related to the issue:
  - Was there a manual or automatic software update?
  - Was a large amount of new data uploaded?
  - Was the site recently migrated from another host?
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can the problem be reproduced?">
  If the issue is intermittent or consistent, tell us:
  - When did the problem first appear?
  - Does it happen from multiple locations or devices?
  - Does it occur across multiple browsers?
  - Does it happen at specific times of day?
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is this an email delivery issue?">
  If your ticket is about email not sending or receiving correctly, we may need the full email headers to diagnose the issue. Copy and paste the complete raw headers (not just the visible From/To/Subject fields) into your ticket message.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can you include screenshots?">
  Screenshots are extremely helpful for visual issues, error pages, and control panel problems. Attach any screenshots that help describe what you are seeing.
</Accordion>

## Viewing Your Ticket History

You can review all tickets you have ever submitted through the client area.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the client area">
    Go to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Manage support tickets">
    Click **Manage support tickets** on the home page dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse your ticket list">
    The **Support** page shows a list of all your submitted tickets with their current status. You can:
    - **Search** for a specific ticket using the search box
    - **Filter** tickets by department, status, or date range
    - **Sort** tickets by date, subject, or other criteria
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a ticket to view or reply">
    Click any ticket in the list to view the full conversation. From inside the ticket you can post a reply to provide additional information or follow up on a response, or close the ticket if your issue has been resolved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Closed tickets remain visible in your history and can be referenced at any time. If a resolved issue reoccurs, you can open a new ticket and reference the old ticket number to give the support team useful context.
</Info>
