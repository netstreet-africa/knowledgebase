Managing your billing at Netstreet is designed to be as straightforward as possible. The client area at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) gives you full control over invoices, payment methods, and renewal settings for every service on your account. The questions below cover the billing scenarios our customers ask about most often.

<Accordion title="How do I manage billing at Netstreet?">
  You manage billing for your account through the client area at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za). After logging in, navigate to the **Billing** section from the main menu to view recent orders, outstanding invoices, and your saved payment methods. You can also manage billing for a specific service by clicking **Manage** next to that product and selecting the **Billing** tab.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do automatic renewals work?">
  Almost all services offered by Netstreet are subscriptions that renew at regular intervals. Domains renew annually, while web hosting plans can run on monthly, annual, or triennial billing cycles. When you order a service, you choose an initial term. At the end of that term, your service renews automatically unless you have cancelled it.

  Renewal invoices are generated **3 days before your renewal date**. If you have a payment method saved in your account, the renewal charge is applied automatically on that date — you do not need to do anything. This early billing is especially important for services like domain names and SSL certificates, which go offline immediately if they expire without payment.

  If you prefer to pay manually or with a different payment method, you can log in to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) at any time and update your billing details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I switch a service to manual renewal?">
  Certain services — such as domain names — can be set to manual renewal by turning off auto-renewal. This prevents an invoice from being generated automatically, meaning payment will not be taken. However, you then become fully responsible for renewing the domain yourself before it expires. **We recommend manual renewal for experienced users only**, as expired domains can be registered by third parties.

  For web hosting packages, manual renewal is not available. Hosting plans have no hard expiry in the same way as domains, and Netstreet offers generous grace periods for late renewals. If you do not want a hosting plan to renew, cancel the service instead and set it to expire at the end of the current billing term.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change my billing cycle?">
  Yes. By default, your service renews on the billing cycle you chose when you first ordered. If you would like to change to a different cycle — for example, switching from monthly to annual — contact the Netstreet support team and they will be happy to make the change for you.

  Keep in mind that **longer billing cycles generally offer better value**. Paying annually or triennially in advance typically comes with a lower effective monthly rate compared to paying month by month.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I pay late?">
  Netstreet offers generous grace periods if you are unable to pay on time. To see the grace period that applies to your specific service, log in to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za), navigate to the service, and click the **Billing** tab.

  As a general guide:

  - Web hosting packages typically have a grace period of at least **3 days** before the service is suspended.
  - We do not terminate hosting plans until **30 days** after the due date, allowing time for easy reactivation once the invoice is paid.

  If you need extra time in exceptional circumstances, contact our support team and we can arrange an extended due date.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I get the promotional price shown on the website when I renew?">
  Promotional prices displayed on the Netstreet website are new customer sign-up offers. When you renew an existing service, the promotional new-order price does not apply.

  That said, renewing on an **annual, biennial, or triennial cycle** automatically gives you a discounted rate compared to renewing monthly — so committing to a longer cycle at renewal time will save you money over the life of your service.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I renew my service early?">
  Yes, you can renew most services before the renewal date. To do so:

  1. Log in to [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za).
  2. Navigate to the service you want to renew early.
  3. Click the **Billing** tab.
  4. Click **Issue next invoice** to generate a renewal invoice, which you can then pay immediately.

  Paying early extends your service term from the current expiry date, not from the date you pay.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I get a refund if I cancel a product or service?">
  Outside of any applicable money-back guarantee period, Netstreet does not issue refunds for services already provided. If you signed up for a longer billing term in exchange for a discounted rate, that discount reflects your commitment to the term.

  In some cases, unused time on a cancelled service may be credited toward other services on your Netstreet account. Contact our support team to discuss your specific situation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I upgrade my hosting plan?">
  Yes, you can upgrade your plan at any time. The upgrade experience depends on the type of change:

  - **Upgrading within the same service type** (for example, from one shared hosting tier to a higher shared hosting tier) is usually seamless and takes effect almost immediately.
  - **Upgrading between different service types** (for example, from shared hosting to a VPS) may require a manual migration or a new order. In those cases, you may need to re-upload website data.

  Contact the Netstreet sales team for specific advice on your upgrade path before placing an order.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I switch to a plan that is no longer listed on the website?">
  You can always upgrade or downgrade your current plan to any plan that is currently available on the Netstreet website. If you want to switch to a plan that is no longer publicly listed, contact our support team at [my.netstreet.co.za](https://my.netstreet.co.za) and we will let you know what options are available.
</Accordion>
