# Product brief

## Description
Netstreet’s knowledgebase covers hosting, domains, control panels, email and security. Movilo is a Netstreet gym-management product for memberships, member billing, classes, check-in, gates, contracts, website enquiries and branded member portals.

## Primary audience
Movilo help serves gym owners and staff, gym members, and Netstreet platform operators, as described in the supplied v0.1.0 guide.

## Jobs to be done
Set up a gym, its team and memberships; manage billing, classes and member access; join a gym, sign a contract and use the member portal; operate Movilo and bill gyms.

## Motivation
Assumption from the guide: bring a gym’s daily work into one service. The user requested Products → Movilo and a help-centre experience inspired by Fresha, using their uploaded guide as the content source.

## Sources and scope
Content comes from the user-supplied guide.html for Movilo v0.1.0. Preserve its product behaviour and version context. Plan prices in the operator guide are explicitly placeholders, not published pricing. Fresha is a layout reference, not a source of Movilo features, articles or media.
